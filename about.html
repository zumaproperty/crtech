About Us

We build like it's our name on the door.

Castle Rock Tech is a Colorado-grown team of designers, engineers, and operators who've spent careers helping companies move faster without breaking what works.

Our Story

From the Front Range, for the long haul.

We started Castle Rock Tech because we kept seeing the same gap: mid-market businesses get pitched either by giant agencies that treat them as the smallest line on a roster, or by freelancers who can't carry a complex initiative across the finish line.

We sit in the middle on purpose — small enough that you'll know everyone working on your account, experienced enough to ship the kind of work that moves a number on your P&L.

Our name comes from the rock that anchors our hometown — and from how we want to feel to our clients. Steady. Recognizable. Worth building around.

Est. Castle Rock, CO
How We Work

Four values we hold each other to.

Outcomes over outputs

We don't celebrate decks shipped or tickets closed. We celebrate the metric you came to us to move.

Plain language

Small, sharp teams

Two senior people will out-deliver a pyramid of juniors every time. We staff lean and stay close.

Built to hand off
