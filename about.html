About Us

Your neighbors, handling your tech.

Castle Rock Tech is a small studio based right here in Castle Rock, Colorado. We help local businesses show up online and get the technology side of their business handled, without the jargon, the runaround, or surprise invoices.

Our Story

From Castle Rock, for the businesses around us.

We started Castle Rock Tech because we kept seeing the same thing: local business owners getting pitched by out-of-state agencies that don't know their town, or by freelancers who disappear halfway through the project.

We wanted something in between. A small, local shop you can actually call, that gives straight answers and stays accountable to its work. We sit down with the owner, explain things in plain English, and stick around long after launch.

Our name comes from the rock that anchors our hometown, and from how we want to feel to the people we work with. Steady. Recognizable. Worth building around.

How We Work

Four values we hold each other to.

Results, not jargon

You don't pay us for slide decks or status reports. You pay us when the phone rings, the profile ranks, or AI saves you an hour a day.

Plain language

If we said something you didn't follow, we said it wrong. Ask us again. We'll explain it like you're sitting across the kitchen table.

Senior people, every time

When you call, you get the person who actually built your site. No junior hand-offs, no account managers between you and the work.

Yours to run, or hand to us

Want to update the site yourself? We'll show you how. Want us to handle it? That's what our Care Plans are for.
